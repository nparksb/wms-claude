# Proposals register

**What this is:** the single place for findings that were **proposed but not filed**. These are mostly T3 items, which
`wms-triage`'s ticket policy says to propose, never file. Before this page existed they lived only in ticket comments
and plan sections. Nothing guaranteed a decision, and archiving a plan made them invisible. The `archive-plan` findings
gate accepts "proposed" as a disposition **only** if the proposal has a row here.

**How to use it:**
- **Add a row** whenever you propose without filing. One row per proposal, with evidence (a `file:line`, query result or
  measured run), blast radius and cost. A proposal without evidence is a hunch and doesn't go here.
- **Nam reviews weekly.** Each row ends **Filed** (with the ticket), **Dropped** (with a one-line reason) or
  **Deferred** (with a re-check date). Decided rows move to the log at the bottom, so the open table stays short.
- **Sweep debt:** seeded 2026-10-15 from the SBDEV-3736/3755/3775 work and memory. Older proposals still sit in
  individual ticket comments; move them here when you touch those tickets.
