### SBDEV-2492 — replen-order source sync on unit-load move (v1 `e0ff548`)

5. Wrap the four BLOCK_REALIGN controller entries (move-unitload, move-stock, set-on-hold ×2, move-fixed-assignment) in `OptimisticLockRetryTemplate` so a concurrent move-vs-cron `@Version` conflict retries with a fresh persistence context.
