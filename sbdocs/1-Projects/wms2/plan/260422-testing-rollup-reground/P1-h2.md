## 7. Verdict

**Still valid (~70 % of the plan):**
- §1 infrastructure inventory — accurate line-for-line; all five artifacts present and unchanged in shape.
- §2 PG-specific surface area — unchanged; PL/pgSQL, `pg_advisory_lock`, native-SQL landmines all still there.
- §3.1 categorization of the original 18 markers — every class present, every cause unchanged, one line drift.
- §4.2 #1 (PG-lane landlord wiring) — still the correct diagnosis, still open, and now **the most expensive
  single unfixed item in the repo's test estate** (14 disabled classes and counting).
- §4.2 #2/#3 (profile + `TestDataFactory` DONE) — still true.
- §7.1 fidelity risk framing — unchanged and still correct.

**Obsolete / falsified:**
- **"18 `@Disabled` markers"** → 23 in the tree, 61 repo-wide. §3.2 headcount reconciliation needs a full rewrite.
- **"The H2 default lane has no Docker dependency"** (§1.6 #1, §5.5) — **false since ~2026-07-30**. 10 active
  default-lane classes start a `PostgreSQLContainer`. The §5.5 acceptance test cannot pass as written.
- **"§4.3 is an enumerated bound"** — breached by ~21 classes.
- **"Precondition SATISFIED — required, blocking pre-merge check"** (§5.5 note dated 2026-06-22) — **there is
  no CI that runs tests.** `.gitlab-ci.yml:47` skips them; the GitHub workflows only build images. The
  precondition was recorded as satisfied on the strength of a named owner, but the *cadence* half was never
  implementable. Corroborates the standing SBDEV-3195 finding.
- **Effort estimate "~6-11 days for Phases 1-4"** — Phase 2's scope grew from 8 classes to 9 (+`KeycloakServiceTest`),
  and Phase 4 now has to un-disable 14 classes and re-home 10 Docker-using default-lane classes.

**New blockers since June:**
1. **Docker crept into the default lane** (10 classes). Any H2 plan must first decide: move them to the PG
   lane, or abandon the Docker-free-default goal. This is a *design* question the plan does not answer.
2. **The orphan `*IT` lane is now load-bearing debt** — 28 classes, 14 enabled but never executed, 14 disabled.
   Fixing failsafe `<includes>` is a prerequisite for Phase 4 (all 3 SELECT-FOR-UPDATE ITs and 11 of the 14
   SBDEV-2217 classes are `*IT`, so even un-disabling them changes nothing until the lane is wired). The pom
   documents why it cannot be flipped today (`pom.xml:683-706`: 5 outbox ITs die on a Flyway ordering bug in
   `db/v1-to-v2-onboarding/schema`; `SequenceTransactionServiceConcurrencyIT` is flaky by construction).
3. **A second landlord-datasource debt pile** — 27 SBDEV-2099 markers across 26 unit classes, three of which
   record that their own `@Disabled` reason is wrong. Not in the plan's scope at all, but it shares the root cause.
4. **No CI test execution anywhere**, so no phase of this plan can be enforced once landed.

**Recommendation:** the plan is not stale in its facts — it is stale in its *ordering*. SBDEV-2217
(`BasePostgresIntegrationTest` + `@ActiveProfiles("integration")`) and SBDEV-3091 (failsafe `<includes>`) have
overtaken the Category-A rebuild as the highest-value work: together they are ~1 day of wiring that unblocks
14 written-but-never-run acceptance tests, versus 3-5 days to rebuild 9 legacy tests nobody has missed since
2026-02. Re-sequence Phase 4 ahead of Phase 2, restate the Docker-free-default goal as a decision rather than
an assumption, and drop the "precondition satisfied" note until a CI lane that runs tests actually exists.
